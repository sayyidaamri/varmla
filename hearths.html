Warmth in every detail

Glazed stoneware

The outer wall is built from pressed stoneware ribs, fired twice and finished with a reactive persimmon glaze. Dense, heavy tile stores warmth and gives it back slowly.

Solid construction

A welded steel frame, tempered glass and a tip-proof plinth carry the ceramic. Blackened steel bands hold the ribs, so a single tile can be replaced at any time.

How it fits into
your home

01

Comfortable flame

The three-step slide gives you a low glow, a steady fire or a tall flame. The burner sits low, so the light falls on the floor and across the room, not in your eyes.

02

Even warmth

Cool air enters at the plinth and leaves beneath the soapstone cap. The ribs warm slowly and evenly, without hot spots or draughts.

03

Nothing left behind

Plant ethanol burns without ash and soot. Now and then you wipe the glass, and that is the whole routine.

04

Simple placing

No chimney, no permits and no drilling. The sections pass through a normal doorway, so the hearth can follow you to the next home.
