Citrus sinensis

Blood orange fuel

€19.00

Base note: Citrus sinensis

A clear, dry orange without sweetness. It lifts a dark afternoon and goes well with cooking.

Pure plant ethanol, denatured and filtered twice, with 1.5% of natural extract. It leaves no ash and no soot on the glass.

  1. Let the burner cool fully before filling.
  2. Pour the fuel slowly, up to the mark inside the tank.
  3. Wipe any drops from the edge and wait one minute.
  4. Open the slide halfway and light with a long match.
  5. Set the flame height after five minutes, when the burner is warm.
  6. Close the lid to put the flame out. Never blow on it.
  • Use only in hearths and burners made for plant ethanol.
  • Never refill a hot or burning burner.
  • Keep bottles upright, cool and away from children.
  • Keep one metre free in front of the glass.
  • Use in normally ventilated rooms and never leave the fire alone.
