Varmla hearths are made for daily use and tested for a full burn before they leave the kiln room. Glazed stoneware ribs hold their colour and keep giving warmth long after the flame is out.

01

Every burner passes a full five-hour burn in our own test room, with the result written by hand on its card.

02
Handmade
in Sweden

Pressed, glazed and fired by a team of nine. Each rib is fitted by hand, so no two hearths share quite the same surface.
