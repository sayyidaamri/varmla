My grandmother’s house had a tiled stove in every room. When I moved to the city I had radiators and a view of other people’s chimneys. I missed the sound of the house going quiet around a flame.

Nils Ahlberg, founder of Varmla

The idea behind Varmla

Get a consultation
Get a consultation

So I went back to clay. It took four winters of broken tiles to find a rib that holds heat, a glaze that keeps its colour and a burner that asks for no chimney at all.

Designed for indoor use

The principles behind Varmla

01

Simplicity

A fire should be easy. We remove every part that does not warm, hold or protect, and then we stop.

02

Listening

Our second door hinge came from a customer in Turku. Letters to the workshop shape the next batch of tiles.

03

Skill

The team has spent decades with clay and kilns. Two of us still press ribs the way our teachers did.

04

Quality

Every hearth burns for five hours in the test room. If the flame is not calm, it does not leave.

Our vision

01

Your own fireside

A real flame right in your apartment. No chimney sweep, no wood store and no drive to a cabin: the evening starts where you already are.

02

Slow by design

An hour by the fire is an hour without a screen. We build things that ask you to sit down.

03

Made once

A hearth should outlive its first owner. Every tile, hinge and band can be repaired or replaced.

We use dense northern stoneware and a reactive persimmon glaze that pools along the ribs. It does not fade, it does not scratch easily and it holds warmth long after the flame.

Our mission

Our mission is to give people in small homes a fire worth sitting down for, made slowly and made once.
