Wood stove vs. flueless hearth: which one is right for you?

February 3, 2026
Get a consultation

A wood stove and a flueless hearth look related, but they solve different problems.

One is a heating system. The other is an evening.

What a wood stove does best

It heats whole houses, works without power and turns a forest into winter comfort. It also needs a flue, a permit and somewhere dry to store wood.

  • A house with a flue: consider a stove
  • A rented flat: consider a hearth
  • A terrace house with both: ask your chimney sweep

What a flueless hearth does best

It gives you a live flame in a home without a chimney. It lights in a minute, leaves nothing to sweep and can move with you.

It warms the place where you sit and leaves the rest to your radiators.

A stove heats the house. A hearth warms the evening.

Costs over ten years

A stove is cheap to feed and expensive to install. A hearth is the other way around.

If you light a fire every evening all winter, wood wins. If you light it two or three evenings a week, the hearth usually does.

Choose by habit

Think about what you will really do on a Tuesday in November. That is the answer.

Curious how it would look in your room? Meet the hearths or write to the studio.
