Delivery, inspection & insurance

Ask for guidelines

Delivery is made to the entrance of your building. Please arrange help for stairs, or book our setup team.

Check the crate before you sign. If you see damage, note it with the driver and send us a photo. We replace any broken tile at our cost.

Once the delivery is signed for, the hearth is considered received in good condition. All shipments are insured up to the door.

Returns & exchanges

Ask for guidelines

Every hearth comes with a 30-day money guarantee. Light it, live with it and decide in peace.

If it is not right, write to us within 30 days of delivery. We collect it from your home and refund the full price, as long as the parts are complete and lightly used.

Fuels and tools can be returned within 14 days, unopened. After this time returns and exchanges are no longer possible.
